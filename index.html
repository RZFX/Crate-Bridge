Features

Everything between two libraries.

One tool for the whole move — playlists, cues, grids, and the housekeeping that goes with them.

Restore playlists

Rebuild your crates from a Rekordbox USB export. One folder per playlist, your folder hierarchy kept, ready for Serato.

Folders.m3u8Serato crates

Cues & beatgrids travel

Hot cues, memory cues and beatgrids are written into the copies as Serato tags. Your originals are never touched.

Library diff

See exactly what one library has that the other doesn't.

Serato → Rekordbox

Export crates as a rekordbox.xml with cues and grids.

Duplicates & missing files

Spot double copies and relink lost files from your music folders.

Key converter

Camelot, Open Key and standard notation, side by side.

Private by design

Crate Bridge reads and writes your folders directly in the browser. Nothing is uploaded, and there's no account to create.

How it works

Three steps, no uploads.

  1. 01

    Connect

    Pick your Rekordbox USB and your _Serato_ folder. They're read in place.

  2. 02

    Choose

    Select playlists and a destination. Decide whether to embed cues and write Serato crates.

  3. 03

    Bridge

    Tracks are copied, tags written, crates created. Restart Serato and play.
